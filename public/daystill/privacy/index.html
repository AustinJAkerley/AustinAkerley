Privacy Policy for DaysTill

Last updated: 2026-07-17

DaysTill is an app for counting down the days until anything — a goal, a trip, or a deadline. Your privacy is the default: everything happens on your own device.

What data we collect

None. DaysTill does not collect, store on servers, transmit, or sell any personal information.

How your data is handled

  • The countdowns you create (their titles, target dates, and colors) are stored entirely on your device using local storage.
  • Your data is never uploaded and is not sent to any backend or third-party service.
  • No account, login, or cloud sync is used.
  • The app works fully offline.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all countdowns and preferences from your device.

Analytics, tracking, and advertising

DaysTill contains no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising. There are no cookies, fingerprinting, or telemetry of any kind.

Permissions

DaysTill requests no sensitive permissions. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, or files.

Children's privacy

DaysTill does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the URL below with a new "Last updated" date.
